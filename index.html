My Unofficial Local Guide to Snowboarding

Step 1

Balance



Go on a flat area of snow and try to balance. Bend your knees slightly, one arm infront and one behind. Try to stay on your heels when you're in a "resting" form. Going on your toes will make you tip over and fall.

Step 2

Going down & control



Stay calm and keep your weight on your heels. Not too much but just enough to keep going. To slow down while going downhill, push your back leg to the front by sliding it forward. It might take a few tries but once you're body is facing down the hill with your feet infront of you, you got it. Now, press all your weight onto your heels to slow down.


To control where you go, your back leg will help again. To turn, move your back leg with your toes to turn. Make sure to not go too much on your toes or else you will fall.

Step 3

Speed



When going down hill you need to learn how to slow down. To slow down you can use the same fundimentals for turning but try to go as close to a stop than a turn. You must combine the two. If you want to go down super fast, bend your knees and get down a bit lower than usual. Dont lift your front feet and keep your feet grounded.

Make sure to...

  • Wear a helment
  • Tighten your snowboarding boots
  • Proerly learn how to fall before going down
  • Be aware of your surroundings
  • Use the bunny slope to practice if you are a beginner
